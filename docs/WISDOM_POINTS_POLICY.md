# Wisdom Points Policy

Wisdom Points are platform credits for eligible Student accounts on Sophis Central. Lecturer accounts do not receive a Wisdom Points wallet, starter allocation, referral rewards, resource-upload rewards, or AI point allocation.

## Student rewards

| Qualifying activity | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Starter allocation | +10 WP |
| Successful Student referral | +15 WP |
| Approved academic resource contribution | +10 WP |

The 10 WP starter allocation is credited once when a Student wallet is first initialized. A referral reward is credited to the referring Student only after the referred Student verifies their email, completes academic onboarding, and meets the platform's academic-profile eligibility checks. The referred Student receives their ordinary starter allocation; there is no additional referral bonus for the referred account.

An academic resource contribution earns 10 WP only after the submission is approved. Pending, rejected, or otherwise ineligible submissions do not earn the reward. The reward is credited to the eligible Student contributor.

## Sophis AI usage

| Successful AI activity | Student charge |
| --- | ---: |
| Ask a question without selecting an academic resource | −1 WP |
| Ask a question using a selected approved academic resource | −2 WP |
| Generate an academic resource summary | −5 WP |

The resource-based question rate applies when an approved academic resource is selected and included as context for the request. Editing and resubmitting a question follows the same 1 WP or 2 WP rate according to whether the conversation uses an approved resource.

Points are deducted only after a successful AI generation. Failed generations are not charged. Students must have enough available points before a request can be completed.

## Account eligibility and records

- Wisdom Points wallets and point transactions are restricted to Student accounts.
- Lecturer accounts are not allocated or charged Wisdom Points.
- Referral rewards are one-time per eligible referred Student and are subject to account and academic-profile checks.
- Starter, referral, approved-resource, and AI transactions are recorded in the Student wallet history.
- The platform may hold suspicious referral activity for review before issuing points.

This policy describes the current Wisdom Points allocations and usage charges. Other proposed earning activities are not included.
