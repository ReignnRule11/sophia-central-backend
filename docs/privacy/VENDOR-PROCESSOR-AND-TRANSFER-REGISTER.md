## Required evidence per vendor

- Legal entity name, address, role and service scope.
- Signed data processing agreement and applicable security schedule.
- Complete current subprocessor list and change-notification process.
- Data-at-rest/in-transit controls, access control, audit logging and independent assurance.
- Exact regions for primary data, backups, logs, support access and disaster recovery.
- Retention, deletion, export and post-termination deletion certification.
- Incident notification deadline, cooperation and evidence preservation commitments.
- Government request disclosure process and transparency terms.
- International transfer mechanism, supplementary safeguards and transfer risk assessment where required.
- AI-specific restrictions on training, retention, human review and onward disclosure.

Do not mark a provider approved based only on its product name or public marketing page. Confirm the actual account, plan, region, enabled feature and signed terms.
